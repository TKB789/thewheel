    Log your Fidelity numbers

    Fidelity doesn't give you its history, so the study can only test what you record. Log the analyst score and S-score when you sell on Monday and Thursday, or every trading day if you can. Once 20 days are logged, they're tested against the next two days' price move.

    Open fidelity_log.csv on GitHub

    How it decides. Calls are recommended near 0.20 delta (0.15 when the stock is running hot), the common range for covered calls when you want to keep your shares. Wheel puts target 0.25 delta. Expirations that span an earnings report are skipped. "Chance of assignment" is the market-implied probability the option finishes in the money, from Black-Scholes using each option's own implied volatility.

    Data comes from Yahoo Finance through a scheduled job in this repository, delayed at least 15 minutes and only as fresh as the last run. Confirm the quote in your brokerage before trading. This is a decision aid, not financial advice.
